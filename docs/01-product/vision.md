# Product Vision

KanbanFlow gives any team a visual, always-in-sync home for their work: open a board and instantly see what is being done, by whom, and what's next. The core interaction — dragging a card across the board — should feel immediate and trustworthy, whether you're the only person on the board or ten teammates are editing it at the same time.

We win by being fast to start (create a board in seconds), fast to use (drag-and-drop over menus and forms), and fast to trust (realtime sync, no "did my change save?" uncertainty).
