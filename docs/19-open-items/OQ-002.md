# Open Question

## Question
Is the current "reconcile-and-toast" conflict resolution for concurrent card moves (`BR-CARD-001`) good enough long-term, or will frequent concurrent editing on busy boards need a richer UX (e.g. live cursors/presence, explicit merge prompts)?

## Why It Matters
At MVP scale, conflicts are expected to be rare. If usage grows to larger, more active teams on a single board, conflict frequency could rise enough to frustrate users with the current simple approach.

## Options / Evidence
No usage data yet to justify more investment; current approach (`FLOW-CARD-MOVE-DRAGDROP`) is the pragmatic MVP baseline.

Frontend Team

Not blocking v1 release.

## Due / Decision Date
Revisit after observing real concurrent-edit frequency in production.
