# Authentication Feature Standard

This standard applies whether AUTH is generated from scratch or imported from a Capability Pack.

Minimum topics: login/logout, credential or identity-provider policy, account recovery, session/token lifecycle, brute-force protection, rate limiting, audit, privacy-preserving errors, secure storage/cookie policy, revocation, multi-device/session behavior, and test coverage. Optional topics include registration, social login, MFA, OTP and email verification. Concrete durations, identity domains and role models are project decisions.
