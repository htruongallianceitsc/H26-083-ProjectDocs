# Reuse Promotion Policy

## Promotion path
`Template -> Standard -> Pattern Pack -> Capability Pack`

## Suggested thresholds
- First occurrence: Standard only.
- Second occurrence with 40%+ similarity: consider Pattern Pack.
- Third or later occurrence with 70%+ similarity and stable semantics: consider Capability Pack.
- Security-critical or cross-cutting infrastructure may be promoted earlier when a shared baseline materially reduces risk.

## Demotion
If a pack accumulates many project-specific overrides or frequent conflicts, demote it to a Pattern Pack or Standard. Reuse quality matters more than pack count.
