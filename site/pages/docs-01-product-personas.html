Personas

Minh — Individual Contributor

A developer on a 6-person team. Opens the board every morning, drags his cards from "To Do" to "In Progress", comments when blocked, checks off work by moving cards to "Done". Rarely touches workspace/board settings.

Lan — Team Lead / Board Owner

Creates boards per project/sprint, defines Lists that match the team's workflow, creates labels (Bug/Feature/Urgent), assigns cards to teammates, and archives a board when a project wraps up.

Huy — Workspace Admin

Sets up the Workspace when the team adopts the product, invites teammates, promotes a trusted teammate to admin, and removes members who leave the team.
