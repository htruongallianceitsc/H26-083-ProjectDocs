Prompt 35 — Document Freshness Review

Given a stale entity and its changed dependencies, review the canonical documentation and determine whether the entity content remains correct.

Do not merely refresh metadata. Compare the changed Requirement/Rule/Screen/API/DB/Test/Request against the tracked document, update the document if necessary, then reconcile freshness with a review note.
